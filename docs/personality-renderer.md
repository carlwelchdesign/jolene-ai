# Personality renderer boundary

`JOL-PER-004C` establishes a non-activating, deterministic boundary between
grounded response substance and personality presentation.

The structured payload owns the source-linked response summary and claims,
citations, limitations, next actions, completion state, and permission state.
Its SHA-256 fingerprint is calculated before presentation. Neutral and Jolene
modes render the same exact semantic segments and fingerprint; the Jolene mode
may add at most one separately tagged presentation segment.

The renderer supports the plan's eleven situational classes. Ornament is
suppressed for overwhelm, grief or acute pain, conflict, error ownership,
high-stakes guidance, urgent incidents, public or shared channels, and voice.
High-stakes and urgent responses receive no personality preface. Every render
keeps completion and permission state explicit.

The offline paired evaluation fails on semantic changes, hidden completion or
permission state, excess or mistimed ornament, celebrity identity or
endorsement language, borrowed biography or quote framing, exaggerated dialect,
and default intimacy.

This slice does **not** connect the renderer to the model, API, Slack, public
delegate, or voice. It does not approve the research corpus or final behavior
specification. Runtime activation remains gated on Carl's relevance and tuning
decision, the complete corpus, rights review, broader evaluation fixtures,
representative human review, and an explicit rollout decision.
